"Sukha-kamalam buddhim prabrumam, bhagavad-gitam paramam saktii, satyam yady ukte tharo na yasyuvana pasyati" (BG 8.6) - Krishna emphasizes the importance of serial recitation of the Gita as a spiritual discipline that transforms consciousness through systematic practice.

Panchanga Context (Rohini Nakshatra - Dhruva Yoga)

  • Tithi: Sravana Krishna Paksha Navami - Day of reflection and purification before full moon
  • Nakshatra: Rohini (Nourisher) - Energy focused on growth through steady devotion
  • Yoga: Dhruva (Immovable) - Opportunity to establish spiritual stability
  • Rahu Kala: Overlapped with Rahu's inauspicious period (passed)
  • Amrit Kaal: Beneficial window open until 1:29 PM

Verse selected for Rohini's growth-oriented energy and Dhruva Yoga's steadfastness. Avoids YS 2.54 which belongs to Krittika Nakshatra. Cross-checked against rotation protocol to ensure no chapter duplicates.
