Bhagavad-gītā BG 18.6

एतान्यपि तु कर्माणि सङ्गं त्यक्त्वा फलानि च । कर्तव्यानीति मे पार्थ निश्चितं मतमुत्तमम् ॥ ६ ॥
etāny api tu karmāṇi
saṅgaṁ tyaktvā phalāni ca
kartavyānīti me pārtha
niścitaṁ matam uttamam
All these activities should be performed without attachment or any expectation of result. They should be performed as a matter of duty, O son of Pṛthā. That is My final opinion.
Thursday is Jupiter's day — Guru, the planet of wisdom, the guru who dispels darkness. And here Kṛṣṇa delivers the verdict that seals Chapter 18's logic: etāny api tu karmāṇi — "all these activities" (the yajña, dāna, tapaḥ of the previous verse) saṅgaṁ tyaktvā phalāni ca — "renouncing attachment and fruit." The activities are not the problem; the saṅga (attachment) and phala (fruit) are. Kartavyānīti me pārtha — "they should be done as duty, O Pārtha." The verdict is niścitaṁ matam uttamam — "My definite opinion, the highest." The Guru (Jupiter) speaks: the highest teaching is niṣkāma-karma — action without attachment to fruit. Today is Guruvār — Jupiter's day, the day of the Guru, the day of wisdom. The Guru teaches: do the work, leave the fruit. The panchang amplifies: Pūrva Bhādrapadā (the former auspicious feet, the fire) yields to Uttara Bhādrapadā (the latter auspicious feet, the water that cools) at 8:26 PM — the fire that burns becomes the water that heals. Harṣaṇa (joy) yields to Vajra (the thunderbolt, the diamond) at 8:09 PM — the joy that becomes the diamond that cannot be broken. The tithi is Daśamī (Kansa Vadh, the day Kṛṣṇa killed Kaṁsa) — the day the tyrant falls, the day the false ruler is removed. The Guru (Jupiter) is the one who removes the false ruler (Kansa) from the heart. The verse is the Guru's final instruction: saṅgaṁ tyaktvā — renounce attachment, not action. Do the duty, leave the fruit. The Guru's final opinion: niścitaṁ matam uttamam. 🏹

The verse is the Guru's final instruction: saṅgaṁ tyaktvā phalāni ca — renounce attachment and fruit, perform duty. The Guru (Jupiter) is the remover of darkness; Kaṁsa Vadh (Kṛṣṇa killing Kaṁsa) is the day the false ruler falls. The Guru removes the false ruler (Kaṁsa) from the heart. The Nakṣatra transition Pūrva→Uttara Bhādrapadā (fire→water) at 8:26 PM mirrors the verse: the fire of action cooled by the water of detachment. Harṣaṇa→Vajra (joy→diamond) at 8:09 PM — the joy that becomes the indestructible diamond of niṣkāma-karma. No repeat: BG 18.1-18.5 used; BG 18.6 fresh for Day 7. The verse is the Guru's final instruction: saṅgaṁ tyaktvā phalāni ca kartavyānīti — renounce attachment and fruit, perform duty. 🏹

Yoga Sutra Connection

YS I.23 — īśvara-praṇidhānād vā — "Or by surrender to the Supreme Lord." The bridge is exact. BG 18.6's kartavyānīti me pārtha — "they should be done as duty to Me" — is īśvara-praṇidhāna in action. The verse says: perform duty as an offering to Me, without attachment to fruit. This is īśvara-praṇidhāna — the surrender of the fruit of action to Īśvara. YS I.23 says īśvara-praṇidhānād vā — "Or by surrender to the Supreme Lord" — as an alternative path to samādhi. BG 18.6 shows the method: perform duty as īśvara-praṇidhāna, offering the fruit to Kṛṣṇa. The Guru (Jupiter) teaches: saṅgaṁ tyaktvā is the method, īśvara-praṇidhāna is the attitude. The verse is the Guru's final instruction: surrender the fruit, do the duty. 🏹

Sanskrit Terms

  • saṅga (सङ्ग) — attachment, clinging; like the memory leak — the reference that prevents garbage collection
    • phala (फल) — fruit, result; like the return value — the output the function returns
  • tyaktvā (त्यक्त्वा) — having renounced, having given up; like free() — releasing the memory, dropping the reference
  • kartavyam (कर्तव्यम्) — duty, that which must be done; like the required callback — the function that must be called
  • niścitam (निश्चितम्) — certain, definite, settled; like the type-checked guarantee — the compile-time guarantee
  • uttamam (उत्तमम्) — the highest, the best; like the optimal algorithm — the solution with optimal complexity
