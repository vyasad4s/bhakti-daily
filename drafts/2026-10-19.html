Bhagavad-gītā BG 16.7

प्रवृत्तिं च निवृत्तिं चjnaa na vidurāsurāḥ ॥ ७ ॥
pravṛttiṁ ca nivṛttiṁ ca
janā na viduḥ āsurāḥ
Those who are demoniac do not know what is to be done and what is not to be done.
On Navami — the ninth tithi of the waxing moon, the penultimate day before Vijayadaśamī. Kṛṣṇa pierces the veil of delusion: the āsurī nature operates in ignorance (ajñāna), unaware of pravṛtti (duty) and nivṛtti (liberation). The mind is agitated by dualities; the asura recognizes neither action nor abstention, neither dharma nor adharma. Today's panchāṅga amplifies this: Somwar (Monday) rules the mind; Sarvartha Siddhi yoga ("success in all undertakings") operates until 7:17 AM EDT tomorrow — a final auspicious seal before the tithi transitions at 3:20 AM EDT Oct 20. The juxtaposition is stark: the last mahāpuṇya yoga of the Navami cycle lands while the āsurī mind remains "clueless" about dharma's outlines. Ganda yoga ("knot") enters at 3:04 PM EDT, tying the intricate karma that only discernment unties. The verse's stark clarity — ajñāna as the inability to distinguish action from inaction — becomes the very meditation for today. Not to generate confusion but to illuminate: the opposite of vijaya (victory) is ajñāna (ignorance). And the antidote is precisely the next day's verse: daivī sampad. Today we hold the darkness; tomorrow we light the lamp. 🏹

Verse Selection Rationale

Today is Maha Navami — the ninth tithi of the waxing moon, the penultimate day before Vijayadaśamī. The chapter rotation places us in BG 16 (Daivī/Aṣurī Sampad Vibhāga Yoga), the chapter that explicitly divides creation into two streams: the divine and the demoniac. BG 16.7 is the pivot verse: "Those who are demoniac do not know what is to be done and what is not to be done." This verse was not chosen — it was demanded by the cosmic convergence. Maha Navami is the threshold: the last full day of the waxing moon before the victory tithi. Sarvartha Siddhi yoga (until 7:17 AM EDT tomorrow) promises success in all undertakings — but only if one knows what "undertaking" to pursue. The āsurī mind, as this verse states, is clueless: it does not know pravṛtti from nivṛtti, dharma from adharma. Monday's mind-energy combined with Sarvartha Siddhi creates the precise condition the verse describes: the intellect obscured, the duty unclear. The timing is unforgiving: Sarvartha Siddhi ends at 7:17 AM, then Ganda yoga ("knot") enters at 3:04 PM — the knot that binds karma. The question today is sharp: do we act with discrimination (viveka) or remain in the asura's blindness? The verse doesn't moralize; it diagnoses. And the diagnosis, written in the tithi and yoga of the day, is exact.

Yoga Sūtra Connection

YS II.33: saṅkleśa-śeṣābhyāṃ vairāgyam — "Detachment arises from the removal of remaining obstacles."

The daivī-āsurī binary of BG 16 and the kleśa-kṛtā of YS II are two angles on the same dismantling. BG 16 identifies the āsurī condition: ignorance that obscures pravṛtti and nivṛtti. YS II identifies the mechanism: kleśas (avidya, asmita, raga, dvesha, abhinivesha) that coat the buddhi in varṇa (color/appearance), making the discernment impossible. Vyāsa's commentary on YS II.29 states that the kleshas are "obstacles remaining" (saṅkleśa) — they are not ultimately real, but they feel absolutely real when active. The parallel is exact: BG 16's "do not know what is to be done" = YS's kleśa-āvaraṇa (obscuration by kleshas). The sādhana is the same: remove the obscuring, and the action becomes clear. Today's Ganda yoga ("knot") after 3:04 PM is the literal knot of the kleshas tying the discriminative intellect. The sādhana is vairāgya — the detachment that YS II.33 promises when the remaining obstacles are removed. Tomorrow's BG 16.5 (daivī sampad vimokṣāya) is the release: the liberated verdict. Today we sit with the knot; tomorrow we unbind. 🏹
