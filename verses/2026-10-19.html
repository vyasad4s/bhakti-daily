🏹 BG 16.7 | 2026-10-19

 

प्रवृत्तिं च निवृत्तिं चjnaa na vidurāsurāḥ ॥ ७ ॥

 

pravṛttiṁ ca nivṛttiṁ ca
janā na viduḥ āsurāḥ

 

Those who are demoniac do not know what is to be done and what is not to be done. Neither cleanliness nor proper behavior nor truth is found in them.

 

On Navami — the ninth tithi of the waxing moon, the penultimate day before Vijayadaśamī. Kṛṣṇa pierces the veil of delusion: the āsurī nature operates in ignorance (ajñāna), unaware of pravṛtti (duty) and nivṛtti (liberation). The mind is agitated by dualities; the asura recognizes neither action nor abstention, neither dharma nor adharma. Today's panchāṅga amplifies this: Somwar (Monday) rules the mind; Sarvartha Siddhi yoga operates until 7:17 AM EDT tomorrow — a final auspicious seal before the tithi transitions at 3:20 AM EDT Oct 20. The juxtaposition is stark: the last mahāpuṇya yoga of the Navami cycle lands while the āsurī mind remains "clueless" about dharma's outlines. Ganda yoga (knot) enters at 3:04 PM EDT, tying the intricate karma that only discernment unties. The verse's stark clarity — ajñāna as the inability to distinguish action from inaction — becomes the very meditation for today. Not to generate confusion but to illuminate: the opposite of vijaya (victory) is ajñāna (ignorance). And the antidote is precisely the next day's verse: daivī sampad. Today we hold the darkness; tomorrow we light the lamp.

 

🔥 YOGA SUTRA CONNECTION: YS II.33 — saṅkleśa-śeṣābhyāṃ vairāgyam — "Detachment arises from the removal of remaining obstacles." The daivī-āsurī binary of BG 16 and the kleśa-kṛtā of YS II are two angles on the same dismantling. BG 16 identifies the āsurī condition: ignorance that obscures pravṛtti and nivṛtti. YS II identifies the mechanism: kleśas (avidya, asmita, raga, dvesha, abhinivesha) that coat the buddhi in varṇa (color/appearance), making the discernment impossible. Vyāsa's commentary on YS II.29 states that the kleshas are "obstacles remaining" (saṅkleśa) — they are not ultimately real, but they feel absolutely real when active. The parallel is exact: BG 16's "do not know what is to be done" = YS's kleśa-āvaraṇa (obscuration by kleshas). The sādhana is the same: remove the obscuring, and the action becomes clear. Today's Ganda yoga ("knot") after 3:04 PM is the literal knot of the kleshas tying the discriminative intellect. The sādhana is vairāgya — the detachment that YS II.33 promises when the remaining obstacles are removed. Tomorrow's BG 16.5 (daivī sampad vimokṣāya) is the release: the liberated verdict. Today we sit with the knot; tomorrow we unbind.
