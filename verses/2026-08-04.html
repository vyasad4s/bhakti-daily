पश्यादित्यान्वसून्‍रुद्रानश्विनौ मरुतस्तथा । बहून्यदृष्टपूर्वाणि पश्याश्चर्याणि भारत ॥ ६ ॥
paśyādityān vasūn rudrān aśvinau marutas tathā bahūny adṛṣṭa-pūrvāṇi paśyāścaryāṇi bhārata
O best of the Bhāratas, see here the different manifestations of Ādityas, Vasus, Rudras, Aśvinī-kumāras and all the other demigods. Behold the many wonderful things which no one has ever seen or heard of before.
This verse is not about exotic divine spectacles - it's a perception upgrade patch. The key phrase is "adṛṣṭa-pūrvāṇi" (not seen before). This isn't about adding new beliefs to your mental model - it's about upgrading your perceptual firmware to see what was always running in the background. Just as a debugger reveals hidden processes in a system, divine vision reveals the ever-present modalities of prakṛti that the conditioned mind filters out as "ordinary." Today's astronomical alignments mirror this perceptual shift: Revatī (Pūṣan, the navigator) yielding to Aśvinī (the divine physicians) represents the transition from guidance to healing - exactly what happens when you stop interpreting experience through conditioned labels and start perceiving underlying patterns.
