दैवी सम्पद्विमोक्षाय निबन्धायासुरी मता |

मा श‍ुच: सम्पदं दैवीमभिजातोऽसि पाण्डव || ५ ||

 

daivī sampad vimokṣāya
nibandhāyāsurī matā
mā śucaḥ sampadaṁ daivīm
abhijāto 'si pāṇḍava

 

The transcendental qualities are conducive to liberation, whereas the demoniac qualities make for bondage. Do not worry, O son of Pāṇḍu, for you are born with the divine qualities.

 

On Vijayadaśamī — the tenth day of the waxing moon, celebrating victory of dharma over adharma — Kṛṣṇa speaks directly to the warrior's heart. The tithi itself is the verdict: daivī sampad (divine qualities) lead to vimokṣa (liberation); āsurī sampad (demoniac qualities) bind to nibandha (entanglement). No middle ground. The Lord's assurance — mā śucaḥ, "do not worry" — is not comfort; it is a tactical declaration. Arjuna's hesitation on Kurukṣetra was not weakness but discrimination (viveka) — he weighed Bhīṣma and Droṇa against duty. That very discrimination proves his daivī nature. The asura acts from krodha (anger), darpa (arrogance), ajñāna (ignorance) — whim, not śāstra. Today's panchāṅga confirms the warrior frequency: Maṅgalavāra (Mars-day) with Dhaniṣṭhā nakṣatra (also Mars-ruled, deity Vasus, "wealth of the shining ones") after 8:32 AM — double Mars, double steel. Ganda yoga (after 3:19 PM) means "knot" — the tangled karma that only the sword of discrimination cuts. The message is clear: your birth is daivī; your duty is the path; the victory is already written in the tithi. Worry is the asura's shadow. The daivī moves.

 

🔥 YOGA SUTRA CONNECTION: YS II.1 — tapaḥ-svādhyāyeśvara-praṇidhānāni kriyā-yogaḥ — "Kriyā-yoga, the yoga of action, consists of tapas (discipline/austerity), svādhyāya (self-study/scriptural study), and īśvara-praṇidhāna (surrender to the Lord)." The daivī sampad of BG 16.1-3 and the kriyā-yoga of YS II.1 are the same architecture viewed from two darśanas. BG 16 lists twenty-six qualities; Patañjali distills them to three operational pillars. Tapas ↔ tapaḥ, damaḥ, tejaḥ, dhṛtiḥ, śaucam — the fire that purifies. Svādhyāya ↔ jñāna-yoga-vyavasthitiḥ, svādhyāyaḥ, satyam — the blade that discriminates. Īśvara-praṇidhāna ↔ abhayam, yajñaḥ, śaraṇāgati implicit in abhijātasya — the anchor that holds when the storm hits. Vyāsa's commentary on YS II.1 states that kriyā-yoga is for one whose mind is not yet fixed in samādhi — the sādhaka in the trenches. That is Arjuna on Kurukṣetra. That is the practitioner on Vijayadaśamī. The daivī qualities are not ornamental; they are the kriyā-yoga toolkit. Today's Ganda yoga ("knot") after 3:19 PM is the precise moment the threefold practice cuts through. The warrior does not philosophize; he executes the sādhana.
