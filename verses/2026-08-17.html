🏹 BG 11.7 | 2026-08-17

 

इहैकस्थं जगत्कृत्स्नं पश्याद्य सचराचरम् । मम देहे गुडाकेश यच्च‍ान्यद्‍द्रष्टुमिच्छसि ॥ ७ ॥

ihaika-sthaṁ jagat kṛtsnaṁ
paśyādya sa-carācaram
mama dehe guḍākeśa
yac cānyad draṣṭum icchasi

 

O Arjuna, whatever you wish to see, behold at once in this body of Mine! This universal form can show you whatever you now desire to see and whatever you may want to see in the future. Everything – moving and nonmoving – is here completely, in one place.

 

No one can see the entire universe while sitting in one place. Even the most advanced scientist cannot see what is going on in other parts of the universe. But a devotee like Arjuna can see everything that exists in any part of the universe. Kṛṣṇa gives him the power to see anything he wants to see, past, present and future. Thus by the mercy of Kṛṣṇa, Arjuna is able to see everything.

 

🔥 This sutra teaches that vivekinah (the discriminative one) sees beyond surface pleasure to recognize the inherent sorrow in all conditioned existence — exactly what Arjuna begins to perceive in the universal form. The ability to see both moving and nonmoving in one place is the mark of the viveki who understands that puruṣa (consciousness) remains unchanged while prakṛti (nature) dances in endless forms.

 

Further, this vision connects to Yoga Sutra 1.2 (yogaś citta-vṛtti-nirodhaḥ — yoga is the cessation of mental modifications). When Arjuna sees the totality of existence in Krishna's body, his mental fluctuations cease — he experiences yoga as direct perception of the unified field.

 

TERM DEFINITIONS:

ihaika-sthaṁ (adverb) - in one place

jagat (masc. noun) - universe, world

kṛtsnaṁ (adjective) - complete, whole, entire

sa-carācaram (compound) - moving and non-moving

dehe (locative sing. of 'deha') - in the body

guḍākeśa (masc. noun) - O Arjuna (epithet meaning "lord of sleep" or "one with thick hair")

yac cānyad (pronoun + adjective) - whatever else

draṣṭum (infinitive verb) - to see, to behold

icchasi (2nd person sing. verb) - you wish, you desire
