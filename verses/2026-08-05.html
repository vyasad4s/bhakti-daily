Bhagavad Gita Chapter 11, Verse 22

रुद्रादित्या वसवो ये च साध्या विश्वेऽश्विनौ मरुतश्चोष्मपाश्च । गन्धर्वयक्षासुरसिद्धसङ्घा वीक्षन्ते त्वां विस्मिताश्चैव सर्वे ॥ २२ ॥

 

rudrādityā vasavo ye ca sādhyaḥ
viśve ‘śvinau marutaś coṣmapāś ca
gandharva-yakṣāsura-siddha-saṅghā
vīkṣante tvāṁ vismitāś caiva sarve

 

All the various manifestations of Lord Śiva, the Ādityas, the Vasus, the Sādhyas, the Viśvedevas, the two Aśvinīs, the Maruts, the forefathers, the Gandharvas, the Yakṣas, the Asuras and the perfected demigods are beholding You in wonder.

 

Today's cosmic alignment isn't accidental - the universe is staging a full attendance roll call. Aśvinī nakshatra (the divine physicians) is yielding to Bharaṇī (Yama's star of restraint) while Kalashtami observes the fierce Saptami→Ashtami tithi transition. In this crucible of time and discipline, Krishna reveals that ALL cosmic beings - not just a select few - are stationed as witnesses to the universal form.

 

This isn't about special visions for special souls. The Rudras (Śiva's fierce aspects), Ādityas (solar principles), Vasus (material substrates), Sādhyas (perfectible beings), Viśvedevas (all gods), Aśvinīs (healing twins), Maruts (storm deities), forefathers, Gandharvas (celestial musicians), Yakṣas (nature spirits), Asuras (power-seekers), and Siddhas (perfected beings) - every category of created intelligence is positioned as a spectator. The wonder (vismaya) isn't reserved for yogis in caves; it's the natural response when any conscious entity confronts the totality of divine manifestation.

 

The Bharaṇī nakshatra's Yama-energy demands we stop selectively perceiving divinity only in "approved" forms. Today's vision strips away spiritual consumerism - you don't get to choose which manifestations of the divine are "worthy" of your attention. The accountant, the traffic jam, the difficult conversation - each contains the same divine intelligence that makes the galaxies spin. The wisdom isn't in seeking rare visions, but in recognizing that what you're already perceiving IS the divine play - if your perception isn't clouded by preference and aversion.

 

YOGA SUTRA CONNECTION

YS I.44: etayaiva savicārā nirvicārā ca sūkṣma-viṣayā vyākhyatā - "By this [samādhi], gross and subtle objects are explained." The sutra describes how through saṁyama on objects, the yogi perceives them as consisting of tanmātras (subtle essences) - "an experience of external reality that is cosmic in scope." This directly parallels BG 11.22 where Arjuna perceives ALL cosmic beings (Ādityas, Vasus, Rudras, etc.) not as separate deities but as manifestations within the one universal form.

YS I.43: kṣīṇa-vṛtter abhijātasyeva maṇiḍ grahītāgrahītaḥ viṣayāṇi tat-tha tad-āṭmino nihitaḥ svabhāvaḥ - "Upon the cessation of mental modifications, the mind becomes like a transparent crystal, able to assume whatever form is presented to it." The wonder (vismaya) of the divine beings in BG 11.22 occurs not through added perception but through the removal of obstructions (vṛtti-nirodha) that normally prevent recognition of the divine in all forms.

YS III.51: ṣaḍ-aiśvarya-pūrṇaḥ puruṣaḥ śāntiḥ - Warning against attachment to siddhis (powers). The divine beings' wonder (vismaya) in BG 11.22 is a natural response, not a goal to pursue. True yoga transcends even cosmic vision to rest in the seer's own nature (purusa-śūnyaṁ citṣaktiḥ).

 

PRACTICE FOR TODAY

7:58 AM - 11:12 AM (Sūla yoga window): Before the tithi shift, practice perceiving resistance not as obstruction but as intelligent feedback. When you encounter delay, difficulty, or contradiction, ask: "What intelligence is communicating through this resistance?" Not to eliminate it, but to recognize its participation in the divine order.

11:12 AM - 11:48 AM (Saptami→Ashtami transition): During this 36-minute window, observe how your perception shifts when you stop categorizing experiences as "spiritual" or "mundane." Notice the same divine intelligence in your breath, your thoughts, your environment - without filtering.

11:48 AM - 6:00 PM (Bharaṇī nakshatra): Under Yama's star of restraint, practice perceiving limitations not as prison but as definition. The boundaries that give shape to experience - time, form, relationship - are not obstacles to divinity but its very medium. What if your limitations aren't blocking God but revealing God?

Throughout the day: Select three different "types" of people you encounter (e.g., authority figure, service worker, stranger, loved one). For each, consciously recognize: "This being, in their unique form and function, is beholding the same divine totality that I am." Not as metaphor - as direct perception of shared participation in cosmic lila.

 

TERM DEFINITIONS:

vīkṣante (present participle) — are beholding, observing, witnessing

vismitāḥ (past participle) — wondering, astonished, amazed

sarve (adjective plural) — all, every, the whole
