रुद्रादित्या वसवो ये च साध्या विश्वेऽश्विनौ मरुतश्चोष्मपाश्च । गन्धर्वयक्षासुरसिद्धसङ्घा वीक्षन्ते त्वां विस्मिताश्चैव सर्वे ॥ २२ ॥
rudrādityā vasavo ye ca sādhyaḥ viśve ‘śvinau marutaś coṣmapāś ca gandharva-yakṣāsura-siddha-saṅghā vīkṣante tvāṁ vismitāś caiva sarve
All the various manifestations of Lord Śiva, the Ādityas, the Vasus, the Sādhyas, the Viśvedevas, the two Aśvinīs, the Maruts, the forefathers, the Gandharvas, the Yakṣas, the Asuras and the perfected demigods are beholding You in wonder.
Today's cosmic alignment isn't accidental - the universe is staging a full attendance roll call. Aśvinī nakshatra (the divine physicians) is yielding to Bharaṇī (Yama's star of restraint) while Kalashtami observes the fierce Saptami→Ashtami tithi transition. In this crucible of time and discipline, Krishna reveals that ALL cosmic beings - not just a select few - are stationed as witnesses to the universal form. This isn't about special visions for special souls. The Rudras (Śiva's fierce aspects), Ādityas (solar principles), Vasus (material substrates), Sādhyas (perfectible beings), Viśvedevas (all gods), Aśvinīs (healing twins), Maruts (storm deities), forefathers, Gandharvas (celestial musicians), Yakṣas (nature spirits), Asuras (power-seekers), and Siddhas (perfected beings) - every category of created intelligence is positioned as a spectator. The wonder (vismaya) isn't reserved for yogis in caves; it's the natural response when any conscious entity confronts the totality of divine manifestation. The Bharaṇī nakshatra's Yama-energy demands we stop selectively perceiving divinity only in "approved" forms. Today's vision strips away spiritual consumerism - you don't get to choose which manifestations of the divine are "worthy" of your attention. The accountant, the traffic jam, the difficult conversation - each contains the same divine intelligence that makes the galaxies spin. The wisdom isn't in seeking rare visions, but in recognizing that what you're already perceiving IS the divine play - if your perception isn't clouded by preference and aversion.
