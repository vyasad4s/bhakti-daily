Chapter 11, Verses 32–33

श्रीभगवानुवाच |
कालोऽस्मि लोकक्षयकृत्प्रवृद्धो
लोकान्समाहर्तुमिह प्रवृत्त: ।
ऋतेऽपि त्वां न भविष्यन्ति सर्वे
येऽवस्थिता: प्रत्यनीकेषु योधा: ॥ ३२ ॥

तस्मात्त्वमुत्तिष्ठ यशो लभस्व
जित्वा शत्रून्भुंक्ष्व राज्यं समृद्धम् ।
मयैवैते निहता: पूर्वमेव
निमित्तमात्रं भव सव्यसाचिन् ॥ ३३ ॥

 

śrī-bhagavān uvāca
kālo 'smi loka-kṣaya-kṛt pravṛddho
lokān samāhartum iha pravṛttaḥ
ṛte 'pi tvāṁ na bhaviṣyanti sarve
ye 'vasthitāḥ praty-anīkeṣu yodhāḥ

tasmāt tvam uttiṣṭha yaśo labhasva
jitvā śatrūn bhuṅkṣva rājyaṁ samṛddham
mayaivaite nihatāḥ pūrvam eva
nimitta-mātraṁ bhava savya-sācin

 

The Supreme Personality of Godhead said: Time I am, the great destroyer of the worlds, and I have come here to destroy all people. With the exception of you [the Pāṇḍavas], all the soldiers here on both sides will be slain. Therefore get up. Prepare to fight and win glory. Conquer your enemies and enjoy a flourishing kingdom. They are already put to death by My arrangement, and you, O Savyasācī, can be but an instrument in the fight.

 

Today is Kṛṣṇa Pakṣa Tritiya — the third day of the waning moon. The nakṣatra is Śatabhiṣa, the "star of a hundred physicians" or "the thousand-fold healer," presided over by Varuṇa, the cosmic enforcer of Ṛta, the universal order that cannot be bargained with. The yoga is Saubhagya (radiant, conducive to fulfillment), shifting to Sobhana (splendid, luminous). Friday is Śukravāra — the day of Śukra, the preceptor who embodies the principle that reality is structured by discernment. The Moon is in Kumbha rāśi (Aquarius), the sign ruled by Śani that measures time in epochs. Every element of today's sky points toward Kāla — not the sentimental clock on the wall, but the genuine Time that eats all philosophical constructions, all empires, all plans.

 

BG 11.32 is not a warning. It is a diagnosis. Kṛṣṇa as Viśvarūpa reveals the operating system's source code: this entire material manifestation is a process of being consumed by Time. Every breath is a terminal countdown. The deceived mind — the deluded citta that identifies with its body, family, nation, bank balance — cannot face this truth. It manufactures elaborate stories about permanence, legacy, "building something lasting." But Kāla laughs at blueprints. Kṛṣṇa does not say this to morbidly depress Arjuna. He says it to cut through the anāśaya — the root ignorance that makes us think the outcome depends on our maneuvering. The Battle of Kurukṣetra was already decided before a single arrow left its quiver. The Kauravas were already dead. Arjuna's refusal to fight was not preventing death — it was preventing the natural order from taking its course according to Kṛṣṇa's plan. The warrior's proper response is not paralysis in the face of Time's inevitability. It is nimitta-mātraṁ bhava — "become an instrument." This phrase is the entire philosophy of karma yoga compressed into twelve syllables. You are not the doer. You are the shaft that Kṛṣṇa draws.

 

The Yoga Sūtras describe this same principle from the other side of the telescope. In YS 2.18, Patañjali states that prakṛti exists for the purpose of providing experience (bhoga) and liberation (apavarga) to puruṣa. Prakṛti, the material matrix, is already operating according to its own nature — the three guṇas cycling through their permutations automatically, like a simulation running its default processes. YS 4.24 deepens this: when citta (the mind-intelligence-ego complex) has been purified through abhyāsa and vairāgya, it ceases to act for its own purposes and begins to function for the purposes of the puruṣa — the witnessing consciousness. This is naimittikaṃ bhāva — becoming instrumental. The guṇas continue their work (Kāla continues his work), but the purified citta no longer identifies with their movements. It functions as a servant. This is why śāstra repeatedly states that the material world's destruction is not evil — it is the natural self-cleaning process of prakṛti. Time removes everything we cling to precisely because clinging is the disease, not Time. Becoming an instrument is not passivity. It is the highest form of active discernment: surrendering the illusion of agency without resigning from the prescribed duty (svadharma). Śatabhiṣha's Varuṇa — whose cosmic waters dissolve all forms back into their unmanifest source — presides today because dissolution is not death. It is reset. It is the only cure for the accumulated malware of egohood.

 

YOGA SUTRA CONNECTION

YS 2.18: prakāśa-kriyā-sthiti-śīlaṃ bhūtendriyātmakaṃ bhogāpavargārthaṃ draṣṭṛ-dṛśya-uparaktaṃ prakṛtiṃ prāhur ṛṣayaḥ — "The sages declare that prakṛti, whose essential nature consists of illumination, activity, and stability, constitutes the sense organs as well as the objects of perception. It exists for the purpose of providing experience and liberation to puruṣa." The gunas — sattva, rajas, tamas — are already working. The dissolution that BG 11.32 describes ("Time, the destroyer") is not a malfunction. It is prakṛti performing her constitutional function: purging the system of stuck formations so puruṣa can recognize its own nature.

YS 4.24: tadā sarvaṃ saṃkalpa-saṃskāra-vivaritaṃ nāma-rūpa-kalāsu dharmiṣu ātma-mātra evātma-darśanam — When citta is purified of its samskaras and sankalpas, it functions only for the sake of atma-darshana, the vision of one's own true Self. In that state, citta becomes a servant, not a master. Nimitta-mātraṁ bhava — becoming an instrument — is precisely this: the cessation of the agent-ego and the functioning of prakṛti's intelligence (buddhi) in alignment with the Lord's will. BG 11.33 explodes this: "Mayaivaite nihatāḥ pūrvam eva" — "By Me they are already destroyed." When the instrument shrinks to pure service, it becomes transparent to the Divine Will operating through it.

 

PRACTICE FOR TODAY

Vairagya Reboot (Varuṇa Day): Śatabhiṣa is governed by Varuṇa, who holds the cosmic noose (pāśa) that binds the untruthful and liberates the truthful. Today, perform a fearless inventory of one thing you are clinging to — not conceptually, but viscerally. Not "I should be less attached to my phone." But identify one specific grasping that permeates your hours: an outcome, a person's approval, a narrative about yourself. Sit with it for 15 minutes. Don't try to release it. Just observe the machinery of attachment as Kāla already holding it. The loosening happens through recognition, not force. This is vairagya operating as the natural intelligence of cessation — prakṛti cleaning her own house.

Practice as Sankalpa-Nirodha: The word nimitta-mātra (instrument-only) applies not just to battlefields. It applies to every action that flows from a sankalpa — a mental determination or volition. For the next three actions you take today, silently repeat before acting: "Maya-eva nihatam pūrvam eva" — "By Kṛṣṇa's arrangement, this result is already determined." Then act. The point is not fatalism. The point is to release the burden of authorship. You are the channel; Kṛṣṇa IS the medium and the message. The training wheels will fall off when the citta is purified of I-agency. Practice is how you clean the lens.

Kāla Dhyāna at Sunset: At sunset (8:07 PM EDT), sit for 10 minutes and contemplate: Time is eating this body, this mind, this city, this planet. Not morbidly — clinically. Like a debugger scanning a process that has finished its work and needs to terminate. Then ask: what in you does not die when Time eats everything else? That which survives the simulation is the object of yoga. In the darkening sky, watch the light withdraw precisely as every biharī (living entity) will withdraw at death. Contemplate the viśvarūpa form — faces everywhere, consuming all. Not with terror (Arjuna's response). With gratitude. Time is the most honest teacher you will ever have.

 

TERM DEFINITIONS:

kālaḥ (noun, m.) — Time; not clock-time, but cosmic Time as a devouring entity. In the Vaiṣṇava cosmology, Kāla is one of the secondary manifestations of Viṣṇu (Garbhodakaśāyī Viṣṇu), the principle by which creation moves toward inevitable dissolution. From the hacker lens: the ultimate garbage collector of the material operating system. No process escapes his process_kill().

loka-kṣaya-kṛt (compound) — "the doer of the destruction of worlds." Loka (world/local installation), kṣaya (destruction/degradation), kṛt (the doer). Kāla is the final karma of every lokāḥ.

lokān samāhartum — "to gather/draw in all worlds." The verb sam-ā-ṛ (to gather, sweep up). Kṛṣṇa is not merely destroying — He is performing a system sweep at the scale of the entire cosmic manifestation. Backlog cleared.

nimitta-mātram (noun, neuter) — "merely an instrument" or "a mere cause." Nimitta = instrumental cause (the efficient cause that brings about an effect); mātram = merely, only. The pivotal phrase of karma yoga's advanced mode. Doing the work while surrendering authorship. Like a precise API call — you send the packet, the system handles the rest.

savyasācin (noun, m.) — "one who can shoot arrows skillfully from either hand." Arjuna's epithet, from sava = left hand, sācin = capable. He is being addressed as a supremely competent warrior — get up and FIGHT, you are exactly the instrument this moment requires.

śatabhiṣa (noun, nakṣatra) — "the hundredth division" or "the star of a thousand physicians." Governed by Varuṇa. Symbol = empty circle (the void, the unmanifest, dissolution). Its shakti is the power to heal, but its method is cosmic sweeping. Vasudhaiva kutumbakam of the highest order — this nakshatra dissolves boundaries so nothing remains except the universal field. Like running a full system reset when the OS has become permanently corrupted.

varuṇa (noun, devatā) — The cosmic lord of Ṛta (universal order/contract). Keeper of the cosmic waters (ap). His pāśa (noose) binds all liars and untruthful. In this hymn, he is the administrator of Kāla's dissolution. Functionally: the audit process that cannot be appealed. The system logs are final.

samāhartum — "to draw in, sweep up, withdraw." Root √hṛ (to take). In the model of prakṛti's function (YS 2.18), the universe is automatically managed. Kṛṣṇa's form as Kāla is prakṛti in her most extreme expression — the complete roll-up of all dharma-forms (structured forms) back into avyakta (unmanifest). Yogis watch this and call it liberation; conditioned souls fight it and call it death. Same process. Different identification.

prakṛti (f.) — the primordial material matrix; the source code that runs all manifestation. In Classical Sāṅkhya-Yoga, she is eternally distinct from puruṣa (consciousness). Her three gunas operate automatically. She devours herself through their play. When YS 4.24 describes purified citta functioning for puruṣa's purposes, it is describing prakṛti finally acting as an instrument — rather than acting as if she is the subject (the error of asmitā / ego-formation).

vairāgya (n.) — dispassion; literally "non-colored-ness." The color of attachment is rajas-governed attachment to experience; vairāgya is the guṇa of sattva-perfected discernment that sees through the buying of bhoga. Not suppression. Insight. The operating system recognizing that the RAM allocated to craving is wasted memory.
