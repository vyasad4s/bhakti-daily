🔥 Yoga Sutra Bridge BG 13.25’s recognition of multiple paths to self-realization finds its classical Yoga counterpart in the Yoga Sutras’ presentation of different approaches to citta-vṛtti-nirodhaḥ (cessation of mental fluctuations): तत्र स्थितौ यत्नोऽभ्यासः .. tatra sthitau yatno'bhyāsaḥ

 

TERM DEFINITIONS:

Sanskrit Terms

dhyāna (ध्यान)

Meditation; sustained focus and contemplation on the divine within. In today’s context of Vishaka nakshatra’s purposeful energy and Vaidhruthi yoga’s discipline, dhyāna represents the meditative path where the aspirant quiets the mind to perceive the Supersoul directly – like a debugger setting breakpoints to inspect the inner state of a running system without altering its execution.
