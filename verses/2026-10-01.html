🏹 Bhagavad Gita Chapter 15, Verse 15 | 2026-10-01

 

सर्वस्य चाहं हृदि सन्निविष्टो मत्त: स्मृतिर्ज्ञानमपोहनं च । वेदैश्च सर्वैरहमेव वेद्यो वेदान्तकृद्वेदविदेव चाहम् ॥ १५ ॥

 

sarvasya cāhaṁ hṛdi sanniviṣṭo
mattaḥ smṛtir jñānam apohanaṁ ca
vedaiś ca sarvair aham eva vedyo
vedānta-kṛd veda-vid eva cāham

 

I am seated in everyone's heart, and from Me come remembrance, knowledge and forgetfulness. By all the Vedas, I am to be known. Indeed, I am the compiler of Vedānta, and I am the knower of the Vedas.

 

Today’s pañcāṅga features Rohini nakṣatra (until evening), symbolizing growth and abundance, combined with Siddhi yoga (until late morning), signifying perfection and accomplishment. Thursday (Guruwar) is ruled by Jupiter, the divine guru, embodying wisdom and the dispeller of darkness. This aligns perfectly with BG 15.15, which declares that the Supreme Lord resides in the heart of every living entity as the source of remembrance, knowledge, and forgetfulness. The Rohini Vrat observance seeks prosperity and growth, which is attained by recognizing the Lord’s presence within as the inner guide who awards knowledge and memory. The Sun in Virgo emphasizes discernment and purity, complementing the sattvic quality of turning inward to the Divine heart. The Siddhi yoga in the morning supports the idea that success (siddhi) comes through devotion to the inner Lord, while the later Vyatipata yoga warns of obstacles that can be overcome by remembering the Lord’s guidance.

 

This verse describes the parametric localization of consciousness: the Supreme Lord (Paramatma) resides as the witness in the heart of every living entity, providing the foundational consciousness that enables remembrance (smriti), knowledge (jnana), and forgetfulness (apohanam). From a Sankhya-Yoga perspective, purusha (consciousness) is all-pervading, yet through prakriti's evolutes—the antahkarana (inner instrument) comprising manas, buddhi, ahankara, and chitta—individualized experience arises. The Lord's presence in the heart is akin to the root user (UID 0) that underlies all processes; smriti corresponds to persistent storage (NVRAM) retaining samskaras across lifetimes; jnana is like the kernel's symbol table that provides essential understanding of system calls; apohanam functions as garbage collection, releasing obsolete mental patterns to prevent leaks and enable fresh perception. The Vedas are the expression of that same divine consciousness, and Vedanta-krta (compiler of Vedanta) represents the structuring of that knowledge into a coherent interface (like an API) for liberation, while Veda-vit (knower of the Vedas) signifies expert root-level access to the source code of reality. Thus, the verse encapsulates the synthesis of devotion (bhakti) and discernment (viveka): turning the mind inward to the indwelling Lord allows one to utilize the same cognitive faculties for liberation rather than bondage.

 

🔥 YOGA SUTRA CONNECTION: YS I.23: īśvara-praṇidhānād vā - "Or [samādhi is attained] by devotion to the Lord."

Just as BG 15.15 describes the Lord seated in the heart as the source of remembrance, knowledge, and forgetfulness, YS I.23 states that devotion to the Lord (īśvara-praṇidhāna) is a means to achieve samādhi. Both emphasize that turning the mind toward the Divine within leads to liberation from the fluctuations of the mind and the attainment of higher knowledge.

 

TERM DEFINITIONS:

hṛdi (हृदि) (locative singular) - in the heart; like the root directory (/) of a system where all processes originate and are governed.

smṛtiḥ (स्मृतिः) (nominative singular) - remembrance, memory; akin to non-volatile storage (NVRAM) that preserves critical data across reboots.

jñānam (ज्ञानम्) (nominate singular) - knowledge, wisdom; comparable to the kernel's symbol table that provides essential understanding of system functions.

apohanam (अपोहनम्) (nominate singular) - forgetfulness, release; like garbage collection that frees up memory by releasing what is no longer needed, preventing leaks.

vedānta-kṛt (वेदान्तकृत्) (nominate singular) - the compiler of Vedānta; similar to the maintainer of a core specification who defines the foundational standards.

veda-vit (वेदवित्) (nominate singular) - knower of the Vedas; akin to an expert with root-level access who understands the complete source code of reality.
