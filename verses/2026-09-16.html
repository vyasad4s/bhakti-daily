🔥 Yoga Sutra Bridge BG 14.26’s declaration that unfailing devotional service leads to transcendence of the gunas finds its classical Yoga counterpart in the Yoga Sutras’ presentation of īśvara-praṇidhāna (devotion to the Supreme Lord) as a means to citta-vṛtti-nirodhaḥ (cessation of mental fluctuations): īśvara-praṇidhānāt vā .. īśvara-praṇidhānāt vā

 

TERM DEFINITIONS:

Sanskrit Terms

bhakti-yoga (भक्तियोग)

Devotional service; the practice of loving engagement with the divine through hearing, chanting, remembering, and serving. In today’s context of Shashthi tithi’s victorious energy and Budhwarā’s intellectual discernment, bhakti-yoga represents the victorious path where the aspirant uses both love and intelligence to transcend material limitation – like a master strategist who combines emotional commitment with tactical precision to overcome a formidable opponent.
