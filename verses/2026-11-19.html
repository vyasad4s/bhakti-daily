🏹 BG 18.6 | 2026-11-19

 

एतान्यपि तु कर्माणि सङ्गं त्यक्त्वा फलानि च ॥ ६ ॥ कर्तव्यानीति मे पार्थ निश्चितं मतमुत्तमम् ॥ ६ ॥

 

etāny api tu karmāṇi
saṅgaṁ tyaktvā phalāni ca
kartavyānīti me pārtha
niścitaṁ matam uttamam

 

All these activities should be performed without attachment or any expectation of result. They should be performed as a matter of duty, O son of Pṛthā. That is My final opinion.

 

Thursday is Jupiter's day — Guru, the planet of wisdom, the guru who dispels darkness. And here Kṛṣṇa delivers the verdict that seals Chapter 18's logic: etāny api tu karmāṇi — "all these activities" (the yajña, dāna, tapaḥ of the previous verse) saṅgaṁ tyaktvā phalāni ca — "renouncing attachment and fruit." The activities are not the problem; the saṅga (attachment) and phala (fruit) are. Kartavyānīti me pārtha — "they should be done as duty, O Pārtha." The verdict is niścitaṁ matam uttamam — "My definite opinion, the highest." The Guru (Jupiter) speaks: the highest teaching is niṣkāma-karma — action without attachment to fruit. Today is Guruvār — Jupiter's day, the day of the Guru, the day of wisdom. The Guru teaches: do the work, leave the fruit. The panchang amplifies: Pūrva Bhādrapadā (the former auspicious feet, the fire) yields to Uttara Bhādrapadā (the latter auspicious feet, the water that cools) at 8:26 PM — the fire that burns becomes the water that heals. Harṣaṇa (joy) yields to Vajra (the thunderbolt, the diamond) at 8:09 PM — the joy that becomes the diamond that cannot be broken. The tithi is Daśamī (Kansa Vadh, the day Kṛṣṇa killed Kaṁsa) — the day the tyrant falls, the day the false ruler is removed. The Guru (Jupiter) is the one who removes the false ruler (Kaṁsa) from the heart. The verse is the Guru's final instruction: saṅgaṁ tyaktvā — renounce attachment, not action. Do the duty, leave the fruit. The Guru's final opinion: niścitaṁ matam uttamam. 🏹

 

🔥 YOGA SUTRA CONNECTION: YS I.23 — īśvara-praṇidhānād vā — "Or by surrender to the Supreme Lord." The bridge is exact. BG 18.6's kartavyānīti me pārtha — "they should be done as duty to Me" — is īśvara-praṇidhāna in action. The verse says: perform duty as an offering to Me, without attachment to fruit. This is īśvara-praṇidhāna — the surrender of the fruit of action to Īśvara. YS I.23 says īśvara-praṇidhānād vā — "Or by surrender to the Supreme Lord" — as an alternative path to samādhi. BG 18.6 shows the method: perform duty as īśvara-praṇidhāna, offering the fruit to Kṛṣṇa. The Guru (Jupiter) teaches: saṅgaṁ tyaktvā is the method, īśvara-praṇidhāna is the attitude. The verse is the Guru's final instruction: surrender the fruit, do the duty. 🏹
