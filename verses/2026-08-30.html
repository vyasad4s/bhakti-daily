adveṣṭā sarva-bhūtānāṁ maitraḥ karuṇa eva ca nirmamo nirahaṅkāraḥ sama-duḥkha-sukhaḥ kṣamīsantuṣṭaḥ satataṁ yogī yatātmā dṛḍha-niścayaḥ mayy arpita-mano-buddhir yo mad-bhaktaḥ sa me priyaḥ

 

adveṣṭā sarva-bhūtānāṁ maitraḥ karuṇa eva ca nirmamo nirahaṅkāraḥ sama-duḥkha-sukhaḥ kṣamīsantuṣṭaḥ satataṁ yogī yatātmā dṛḍha-niścayaḥ mayy arpita-mano-buddhir yo mad-bhaktaḥ sa me priyaḥ

 

One who is not envious but is a kind friend to all living entities, who does not think himself a proprietor and is free from false ego, who is equal in both happiness and distress, who is tolerant, always satisfied, self-controlled, and engaged in devotional service with determination, his mind and intelligence fixed on Me – such a devotee of Mine is very dear to Me.

 

Today's celestial alignment creates a potent nexus of devotional energy. Kajari Teej, observed during Krishna Paksha Tritiya, celebrates the divine love between Shiva and Parvati – the ultimate exemplar of bhakti where the ascetic deity embraces householder devotion. The Moon's transit through compassionate Pisces amplifies spiritual sensitivity and empathic connection, while the Sun in regal Leo ignites the heart's courage to love fiercely.

 

🔥 YOGA SUTRA CONNECTION: YS 1.33 - "maitrī karuṇā muditā upekṣāṇāṁ duḥkhā punyāpuṇyaviṣayāṁ bhāvanam cittaprasādanaṁ"

 

TERM DEFINITIONS:

Adveṣṭā (adjective) - Non-envious, free from malice; Like a rootkit that operates without triggering security alerts – pure devotion runs stealthily in the background of consciousness, elevating all processes without raising the "envy" process flag in the mind's task manager.

Karunā (noun) - Compassion, mercy; The ultimate packet sniffer with zero-latency response – it detects suffering in the data stream of existence and immediately responds with healing packets of kindness, operating at kernel level of compassion.

Samaduḥkhasukha (noun) - Equal in happiness and distress; A load balancer that distributes both pleasure and pain evenly across all servers of experience – no single node gets overloaded with either extreme, maintaining optimal system performance regardless of incoming traffic patterns.

Yatātmā (noun) - Self-controlled, self-mastered; The BIOS/firmware level of consciousness – before any OS (ego) or applications (desires) load, the self-mastered one operates at the foundational level where only essential processes run, immune to user-level crashes or malware.

Mayy arpita-mano-buddhir (phrase) - Mind and intelligence fixed on Me; Setting the CPU affinity mask to exclusively run on Krishna-core – all computational threads (thoughts, decisions, actions) are bound to process on the Divine core, preventing context-switching to materialistic processes that would cause system instability.
