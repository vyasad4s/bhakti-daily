🏹 BG 17.2 | 2026-11-04

 

श्रीभगवानुवाच त्रिविधा भवति श्रद्धा देहिनां सा स्वभावजा ॥ २ ॥ सात्त्विकी राजसी चैव तामसी चेति तां शृणु ॥ २ ॥

 

śrī-bhagavān uvāca
tri-vidhā bhavati śraddhā
dehināṁ sā svabhāva-jā
sāttvikī rājasī caiva
tāmasī ceti tāṁ śṛṇu

 

The Supreme Personality of Godhead said: According to the modes of nature acquired by the embodied soul, one's faith can be of three kinds – in goodness, in passion or in ignorance. Now hear about this.

 

Wednesday is Mercury's day — buddhi, the discriminating intellect. And here Kṛṣṇa hands us the diagnostic: faith is not chosen; it is *inherited* as svabhāva-jā, born of the embodied soul's acquired guṇa-proportion. The fast of Rama Ekadashi strips away the noise — no food to dull the sensorium, no sensory input to mask the signal. What remains is the raw reading: is the faith sāttvikī (clear, sustained, self-referential), rājasī (outcome-hungry, validation-seeking), or tāmasī (inert, borrowed, unexamined)? The panchanga mirrors this: Purva Phalguni (enjoyment, the fruit) yields to Uttara Phalguni (the rear legs — the stability that carries the load). Indra (the king, the wielder) yields to Vaidhruthi (the cautionary, the wrong-way current). The moon in Simha (ego-seat) slips into Kanya (the analyst, the discriminator). This is not theory. The fast is the compiler flag: -O3 for the mind. Strip the dead code. Read the register. The threefold faith is not a menu; it is the architecture you are running on. 🏹

 

🔥 YOGA SUTRA CONNECTION: YS I.20 — abhāve svarūpe śuddhiḥ — "In the absence of the false, the true nature reveals itself." The bridge is precise: BG 17.2 says faith is born of svabhāva (the guṇa-configuration); YS I.20 says when the false (asmitā, the false owner) is removed, the true nature (puruṣa-as-puruṣa) shines. The fast is the absence-maker — it removes the āhāra (food) that feeds the guṇa-loop, creating the vacuum where svabhāva shows its face. And YS II.32's īśvara-praṇidhāna is not the surrender *after* knowing; it is the method *for* knowing. You don't debug by wishing; you debug by running the test suite with the right flags. Today the flag is: no food, only the query. What faith remains when the buffer is empty? That is your svabhāva. 🏹

tri-vidhā (त्रिविधा) — threefold; like a triple-branch switch — the circuit routes power through one of three paths

śraddhā (श्रद्धा) — faith, trust, confidence; like the authentication token — the credential that grants access to the system's inner functions

dehinām (देहिनām) — of the embodied; like per-instance settings — the configuration that applies only to this body-hardware

svabhāva-jā (स्वभावजा) — born of one's own nature; like the default build — the version that ships with the machine, before any customization
