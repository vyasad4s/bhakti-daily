Thursday as Guru-Day: Guruvār is Jupiter's day, the day of the Guru, the day of wisdom. The Saṅkhya lens: saṅga (attachment) is the rāga (attachment) that binds puruṣa to prakṛti; phala (fruit) is the karma-phala that binds to rebirth. Saṅgaṁ tyaktvā is the surgical removal of rāga; phalāni ca tyaktvā is the surgical removal of dveṣa (aversion to fruitlessness). The Guru (Jupiter) is the guru — the heavy one, the one who weighs and removes. Kansa Vadh is the day the false ruler (Kaṁsa, ahaṅkāra) falls.
