 >>> SĀṄKHYA LENS <<<

Saturday as Śani-Day: Śanivār is Saturn's day, the planet that enforces the boundary between puruṣa and prakṛti. The Sāṅkhya lens: tyāga (renunciation) is not a single act but a threefold operation matching the three guṇas. Sāttvika-tyāga (knowledge-renunciation) = sattva; rājasika-tyāga (passion-renunciation) = rajas; tāmasika-tyāga (ignorance-renunciation) = tamas. The threefold tyāga is the structural map of how prakṛti binds or releases puruṣa. Śani (Saturn) is the planet that enforces the boundary between puruṣa and prakṛti — the niścaya is the viveka-khyāti (discriminative discernment) that Saturn enforces: the boundary is real, the judgment is final. 🏹
