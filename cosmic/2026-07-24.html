The tithi is Śukla Ekādaśī from 11:43 PM last night until 2:04 AM two nights from now — a potent window for spiritual practice. These alignments create a field where the material nature (prakṛti) rests while consciousness (puruṣa) remains awake as witness — the essence of Ekadashi fasting and vigil.
