 >>> SĀṄKHYA LENS <<<

Citta-vṛtti Discrimination: The verse describes the citta's response to the Lord's glories. For the siddha whose citta is purified and oriented toward Īśvara, the glories produce anurajyate — attachment, affinity, recognition. For the demonic citta, the same glories produce bhīti — fear, recoil, flight. The stimulus is identical; the response is determined by the prior state of the citta. This is the Sāṅkhya principle of guṇa manifestation: the same external event activates different guṇa-predominant modifications in different beings.

Purusha Recognition: The siddhas bow not because they have been converted but because they have recognized. Recognition (anuśravana → anurajyate → namaskāra) follows the classical sequence of bhakti: hearing → attraction → surrender. The demons' flight is the reverse sequence: anitya → dveṣa → palāyana. What kindles today's field (Ekādaśī + Vyāghāta) is the collapse of the middle option — the neutral, lukewarm, undecided citta. You are either bowing or fleeing; there is no remaining fence-sitting.

System metaphor: A root-level audit that exposes every running process. The legitimate processes handshake with the audit system and are granted elevated access (siddha namaskāra → deeper bhakti). The malicious processes detect the audit and terminate immediately (demon flight). The audit itself (Lord's glories / Vyāghāta) does not discriminate; it simply exposes what is.
