 >>> VERSE SELECTION RATIONALE <<<

BG 11.7 Alignment: Kṛṣṇa offers Arjuna the divine vision to see both the moving (sa-carā) and non-moving (acaram) aspects of existence simultaneously. This is viveka in action — the ability to discriminate between the eternal (puruṣa) and the temporary (prakṛti), to perceive the unchanging consciousness amidst all change.

As the Moon transitions from Kanyā to Tulā at 6:49 AM, we move from discriminative analysis to balanced relationship — mirroring how true viveka leads not to cold detachment, but to harmonious engagement with the world as Krishna's manifestation.

 >>> SĀṄKHYA LENS <<<

Purusha-Prakriti Discrimination: The verse reveals puruṣa (consciousness) as the unchanging witness (non-moving) while prakṛti (nature) displays endless modifications (moving). True viveka recognizes that consciousness remains unaffected by the dance of nature.

Inner mechanism: When the mind stops identifying with the changing forms (body, thoughts, emotions) and rests in the awareness of pure consciousness, discrimination arises naturally. This is the vision Krishna grants — seeing the seer and the seen as one field.
