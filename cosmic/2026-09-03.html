Kalashtami, governed by Lord Bhairava (the fierce form of Shiva), represents the destructive aspect of time that removes fear and illusion. Krishna Janmashtami celebrates the Supreme's blissful, all-pervading presence. The planetary alignment reinforces this duality: Moon in Taurus provides steadiness and nourishment, while Sun in Leo adds royal vitality and majesty. The transitional Vyaghata yoga (obstacle-smashing) until mid-morning clears blockages, giving way to Harshana yoga (joy, prosperity), symbolizing the removal of obstacles to experiencing the Divine's grace. This field invites recognition of the Lord's omnipresence—both as the terrifying destroyer of ego and the sweet, intimate beloved who pervades every atom.
